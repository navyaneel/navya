About

Pursuing dual degrees in Finance (Whitman School) and Applied Data Science (iSchool) at Syracuse University as a Renée Crown Honors and Chancellor's Scholar, I've built four end-to-end Python projects spanning credit risk modeling (XGBoost + Logistic Regression), portfolio optimization (Markowitz MPT, SLSQP), SPY price prediction (stacked LSTM, TensorFlow), and audio feature analytics (K-Means, PCA, Random Forest). Each ships with a live Streamlit dashboard.

On the research side, I co-authored a quantitative study on gender-based pricing disparities — accepted for publication in the GAEE India Journal — using paired t-tests, ANOVA, and regression in R across 26 matched product pairs. I'm currently developing an Honors thesis proposal examining racial disparities in mortgage lending using HMDA data, with a focus on the CFPB's Regulation B disparate impact framework.

Beyond technical work, I oversee a $4M+ annual student activity budget as Finance Board Commissioner for Syracuse's SGA, and contributed investor-facing strategy work for Meteor Venture, an early-stage angel network. Prior to Syracuse, I completed programs at IIM Bangalore and IIFT during my freshman year, with field research on rural economic development in Andhra Pradesh.

Targeting an MFE or MFin program for Fall 2028.
